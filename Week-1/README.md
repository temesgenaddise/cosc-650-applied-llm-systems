## Week-1 Tokenization Assignment

## Purpose
. Quantify the multilingual tax: how many more tokens the same meaning costs in one language than another. 

. Read a tokenizer's behavior at the level of individual splits, not just totals.

. Connect tokenization to cost and context-window budgets.

.Treat your course repository as working infrastructure, not homework storage.

## Steps followed

Amharic/Ethiopian language passage with 100 words was taken and translated using Google Translate.

Tokenization analysis was done using GPT-4 and GPT-4o

## Results found

The Amharic language passage needed way more tokens than the same passage in English.

Multilingual tax: Amharic/English was high.

## Conclusion

Based on the analysis, Amharic language users incur a much higher cost per token than English language users.
